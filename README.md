# IITB Resume Builder

**Use it:** https://iitb-resume-builder.pages.dev/

A free tool for IIT Bombay students: upload your resume, project reports and GitHub, choose the portal or external header, 1 or 2 pages and a target role, and get a resume in the IITB format where every line reaches the right margin.

Not an official IIT Bombay website. It collects and stores nothing: your work stays in your browser, or in your own Google Drive if you sign in.

The old address (kartikya2207.github.io/iitb-resume-builder) forwards to the new one. The source code is private.
